Supported Image Formats

JPG / JPEG

Photographs & Complex Graphics

Standard photography format with lossy DCT encoding. Highly compressible with excellent size-to-quality ratio. Does not support transparency.

WebP

Modern Web Standard

Next-gen Google format offering 25–35% smaller file sizes than comparable JPEGs while supporting both lossy compression and full alpha transparency.

PNG

Logos & Transparent Graphics

Lossless raster format preserving crisp lines, text, and transparent layers. Converting high-resolution PNG photos to WebP can yield up to 80% reduction.

How Image Compression Works in Praxuni

Traditional online image compressors require you to upload your personal photos and documents to remote servers, wait for server-side processing queues, and download them back.

Praxuni takes a modern, browser-first approach. Utilizing the browser's hardware-accelerated Canvas and createImageBitmap APIs, images are decoded, scaled, stripped of unnecessary EXIF metadata, and re-encoded directly in your browser's memory. This delivers instant processing speeds and complete privacy, as your files never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image?

Drag and drop your files into the upload box or click "Choose Images". Select your compression mode or enter a target file size, then download the compressed files once processing completes.

Does image compression reduce quality?

Lossy compression can reduce file size significantly, but the amount of savings and visible quality change depends on the image, format, dimensions, and selected quality. Praxuni's default balanced mode optimizes compression while maintaining high visual clarity.

Can I compress JPG images?

Yes. JPG images are natively supported. You can re-encode them at varying quality levels, reduce resolution dimensions, or convert them into WebP for even greater compression.

Can I compress PNG images?

Yes. Because standard PNG is lossless, browser encoders do not support lossy PNG compression directly without color palette quantization. For maximum savings while preserving transparency, Praxuni recommends converting PNGs to WebP.

Can I compress WebP images?

Yes. WebP images can be compressed directly to smaller WebP files or converted to JPEG where required.

How do I compress an image to 100 KB or 50 KB?

Check the "Target File Size" option in the settings panel and click the 100 KB or 50 KB preset (or type a custom size). The tool uses an iterative binary search algorithm to find the exact quality level that brings the image down to your target.

Why couldn't my image reach the target size?

If an image is very large or complex (e.g. a 24-megapixel photograph), reaching an aggressive target like 20 KB may be mathematically impossible without downscaling pixel dimensions. If you enable downscaling in Advanced Settings or convert PNG to WebP, smaller targets become achievable.

Are my images uploaded to Praxuni?

No. Your images are processed entirely within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and Blob APIs. No image data is ever uploaded to our servers or stored remotely.
