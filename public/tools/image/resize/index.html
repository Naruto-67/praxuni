Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 2D Resize Grid work?

The 2D Resize Grid represents width on the horizontal X-axis and height on the vertical Y-axis. By clicking or dragging the handle, you dynamically adjust the output dimensions. When Aspect Ratio Lock is turned on, moving the handle constrains your movement along the diagonal corresponding to the image's original proportions.

What is Prevent Upscaling?

Prevent Upscaling ensures that you do not accidentally resize an image to dimensions larger than its original resolution. Enlarging raster images beyond 100% can cause noticeable pixelation, blurriness, or softness. If you intentionally want to enlarge an image, simply uncheck the Prevent Upscaling option.

Are my uploaded images private and secure?

Yes, completely. Praxuni is built on a browser-first architecture. Your images are loaded into local browser memory via HTML5 Canvas and Blob APIs. No image data is ever uploaded or transmitted across the network.

What file formats are supported?

Praxuni Image Resizer natively supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, and WebP images. You can also convert between these formats upon export, and transparency is preserved when saving as PNG or WebP.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes! Drag multiple files into the dropzone or click Add More to queue several images. The chosen resize configuration applies across the batch, and you can download all resized files in a single ZIP archive or download each individually.
