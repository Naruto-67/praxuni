Supported Image Formats & Conversion Guide

📱

iPhone HEIC Conversion

Apple devices capture photos in high-efficiency HEIC format by default. Our tool decodes HEIC files directly in your browser's WebAssembly sandbox, creating high-resolution universal JPEGs, PNGs, or WebP files without sending a single byte to any remote server.

🛡️

100% Client-Side Privacy

Unlike traditional online converters that upload your personal documents and private camera photos to cloud servers, Praxuni operates completely within your local browser memory. Even if your internet disconnects, your conversion finishes seamlessly.

⚡

PNG, WebP & JPEG Quality

PNG export produces lossless pixel encoding with full transparency support, though converting from a lossy source (such as JPEG) cannot recover previously discarded data. WebP and JPEG exports use configurable lossy compression where your chosen quality slider directly balances output fidelity against file size.

🖼️

Supported Inputs & GIF Handling

We support inputs in HEIC, JPG, PNG, WebP, SVG, BMP, and GIF formats. Because images are decoded into browser canvas frames, animated GIF inputs are converted as static single frames rather than multi-frame animations. For JPEG and BMP outputs, customizable background color filling cleanly replaces any alpha transparency.
